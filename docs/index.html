Your eval score dropped. Is the model worse, or are the questions harder?

If a release suddenly gets more of a hard question type, the overall pass rate falls even when the model is just as good at everything. Enter your before/after counts per question type and an agent investigates with three measurement tools, then gives a verdict backed by the evidence it collected.

How the agent works

scoreOverall pass rate before and after, with sample sizes.
segmentsPass rate inside each segment, and how much traffic each one got.
mixCounterfactual: the old segment rates at the new traffic mix. Splits the drop into a mix effect and a within-segment effect.
finishVerdict assembled only from the evidence collected.

Guardrails: the first action must be score. A drop under 3 points closes the alert. A bigger drop requires both segments and mix before finish. At most four actions; repeated, unknown or premature actions stop the run. The model only ever sees aggregated rates and the list of legal actions. The decomposition is descriptive: it assumes comparable segments in both windows and does not test statistical significance.
